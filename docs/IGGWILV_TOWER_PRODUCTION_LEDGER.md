# Iggwilv's Tower: Production Ledger

This is the binding checklist for the eleven Foundry VTT maps. The campaign guide is authoritative for progression and item state. The two supplied layout diagrams govern room topology only; they are not visual-style references and may be enlarged for sound tactical play.

## Shared delivery standard

- One distinct gridless battlemap per room; do not combine a floor into one image.
- Foundry grid: square, 5 ft per cell, 64 px per cell, no partial grid columns or rows.
- Art direction: professional hand-painted VTT plan view, true 90-degree overhead, no text, labels, tokens, characters, creatures, baked grid, visible wall faces, perspective, or theatrical shadows.
- Materials: charcoal stone, polished blackwood, black metal, aged brass/gold, controlled burgundy/violet, selective Fey-blue glow. Iggwilv's rooms are refined archfey witchcraft and meticulous scholarship; Graz'zt's obsidian/thorn/sixfold language appears only where the story calls for it.
- Build lived-in rooms with natural functional clusters, tactical cover, and clear paths. A typical route is 10 ft wide; a minimum passable route is 5 ft.
- Doorway safety rule: each normal door has a clear 10-ft by 10-ft receiving area immediately inside it. Never place a removable clue, cabinet, pedestal, large prop, or required interaction directly on the door axis or within that receiving area. Wall art may occupy a nearby alcove but must not be centered over a usable doorway when it is meant to be removed or examined.
- Vertical-circulation rule (user direction supplementing the guide): the central spiral stair is one continuous 20-ft-diameter, two-floor stair shaft. It appears in both Floor 1 and Floor 2 Landings at matching central positions and matching clockwise orientation. Floor 1 depicts the ascent toward Floor 2; Floor 2 depicts the matching lower-floor arrival. It receives one bidirectional stair teleport pair, not separate unrelated stair features.
- Required clue visible means visually readable, not shrine-sized. Hide, lock, or omit all other progression items until the guide says they are available.
- Validate each candidate against its topology, required features, prohibited spoilers, grid footprint, and movement lanes before saving it as final.

## Room ledger

### 1. Floor 1 Landing

- Connections: north Private Chambers, west Curiosities, east Private Library, south Witch's Laboratory. No Study door.
- User override: central main spiral stair; star is a central feature of a northwestern wall sculptural relief, not a floor emblem. The relief is offset from the north Private Chambers door so it cannot block that threshold.
- Show: star relief, modest private vestibule decor.
- Do not show: a Study entrance or any progression rewards.

### 2. Curiosities Room

- Connection: east to Floor 1 Landing only.
- Show: upright aquarium-like tank containing a clearly two-foot, desiccated-but-flesh Nixie; Hourglass Coven hourglass; curated fey/demonological collection, research desk, cases, and cover.
- Do not show: password, Seal, keys, Astral Fruit, or revived Nixie state.

### 3. Iggwilv's Private Chambers

- Connection: south to Floor 1 Landing only.
- Show: bed, portrait of Iggwilv with black crow's-foot facial mark and Sovereign Seal medallion, empty matching socket in frame, portable Sunglobe, foot bath and Epsom salts.
- Do not show: Obsidian Crow's-Foot Key or the Sovereign Seal hidden inside the portrait.

### 4. Private Library

- Connections: west to Landing; north to Private Study; one visually distinctive bookshelf is the secret hidden-library entrance.
- Show: Red Leaf Fern; table prepared for the milk-drinking skeleton encounter but no skeleton creatures; broad shelves and a book-order puzzle shelf.
- Do not show: an open hidden room, candles, or book-puzzle answer.

### 5. Hidden Library Room

- Connection: one concealed bookshelf entrance corresponding to Private Library.
- Show: Black Tallow Candles as stored ritual supplies, in a believable cabinet/rack rather than a pedestal; protected hidden archive decor.
- Do not show: Cambion, Demon Skull, Powdered Silver, or completed ritual.

### 6. Private Study

- Connection: south to Private Library only.
- Show: entry-door relief with hollow star socket; desk with Ingredient Cabinet Key resting openly; riddle statue with a plausible concealed compartment.
- Do not show: Powdered Silver inside the statue.

### 7. Witch's Laboratory

- Connection: north to Floor 1 Landing only.
- Show: central mixing cauldron, mortar/pestle bone-grinding workspace, normal-sized reinforced transparent cabinet in a functional workshop. Per the guide, Astral Water and Demon Skull are visibly stored within its cabinet, but neither is a giant display piece.
- Do not show: cabinet key, completed plant food, or pre-supplied ingredient-puzzle components.

### 8. Floor 2 Landing

- Connections: north Arcane Vault; lower-left/west Summoning Chamber; lower-right/east Astral Garden. Rose door to Floor 3 is an architectural feature in this scene, not a new map.
- Show: Obsidian Crow's-Foot Key openly on a normal-sized trapped mount/pedestal; dormant enchanted swords; closed rose-and-thorn door.
- Do not show: completed Thorn Key or Floor 3 beyond the door.

### 9. Summoning & Binding Chamber

- Connection: one northeast-facing entrance toward Floor 2 Landing.
- Show: large containment ring, empty stations for Demon Skull/Black Tallow Candles/Powdered Silver, assistant workstation, precise binding equipment.
- Do not show: the three supplied components, vampire assistant, Cambion, or Obsidian Thorn.

### 10. Astral Garden

- Connection: northwest-facing entrance toward Floor 2 Landing.
- Show: dormant Astral Treant, three activation stations (Sunglobe/Astral Water/Plant Food), faerie-dragon water source and perches, unfruiting growth point.
- Do not show: faerie dragons, Astral Fruit, awakened Treant state, or plant-food result.

### 11. Arcane Vault

- Connection: south to Floor 2 Landing only.
- Show: two-part entry interface for Sovereign Seal and arcane password (no readable password); secure Ivory Thorn reliquary, used only once the Vault scene is entered.
- Do not show: Sovereign Seal, password, Obsidian Thorn, or completed Thorn Key.

## Foundry scene and teleport criteria

- All eleven maps become separate inactive navigation-hidden Scenes, each with square 5-ft / 64-px grids, 0% padding, dark background, Token Vision and Fog Exploration enabled, Global Illumination disabled for indoor tower spaces.
- Add native Scene Region teleports in both directions for every diagram connection. Source thresholds are one square; arrivals are two squares inside their destination room to prevent immediate return loops.
- Also connect the Floor 1 and Floor 2 central stairs, and connect the existing Castle of Dreams scene to the Floor 1 Landing after inspecting the castle's actual tower entrance. The Floor 3 rose door receives no teleport because Floor 3 is out of scope.
